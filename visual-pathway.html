Reflex arcs

Pupillary light reflex

Retina → optic n. (CN II) → chiasm / optic tract
↓
Pretectal nucleus (dorsal midbrain) → both Edinger-Westphal nuclei (rostral midbrain)
↓
CN III → ciliary ganglion → short ciliary nn.
↓
Sphincter pupillae → miosis (direct + consensual)

Near response / accommodation

Near blur → retina → CN II → LGN → visual + association cortex
↓
Midbrain near-response neurons → Edinger-Westphal (rostral midbrain)
↓
CN III → ciliary ganglion + medial recti
↓
Accommodation + convergence + miosis (triad)

Corneal (blink) reflex

Cornea → CN V1 (nasociliary)
↓
Spinal trigeminal nucleus → both facial motor nuclei
↓
CN VII → orbicularis oculi
↓
Bilateral blink

Vestibulo-ocular reflex (VOR)

Head motion → semicircular canals → CN VIII
↓
Vestibular nuclei → MLF
↓
CN III, IV, VI
↓
Eyes rotate opposite to head → gaze stabilised

Optokinetic reflex

Moving scene → retina → visual cortex
↓
Cortex → brainstem (NOT / pretectum)
↓
CN III, IV, VI
↓
Smooth pursuit + corrective nystagmus

Lacrimation (reflex tearing)

Cornea / conjunctiva → CN V1
↓
Superior salivatory nucleus
↓
CN VII → greater petrosal n. → pterygopalatine ganglion
↓
Lacrimal gland → tear secretion

Oculocardiac reflex

Globe traction / pressure → CN V1
↓
Trigeminal (Gasserian) ganglion → vagal nucleus
↓
CN X (vagus)
↓
Bradycardia / arrhythmia — release traction; ± atropine
