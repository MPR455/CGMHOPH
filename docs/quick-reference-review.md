# Quick Reference teaching review

Review date: 2026-10-25 (Asia/Taipei).

## Coverage and language

- All **475 disease entries** (377 main records and 98 expanded catalogue records) have Traditional Chinese teaching copy.
- All **192 intervention entries** have Traditional Chinese patient counselling centred on the treatment flow (preparation, what happens during treatment, and immediate follow-up), recovery time, prognosis and postoperative care. Repeat treatment and relevant risks are explained, with alternatives available in a collapsed section.
- Disease teaching explains causes/mechanisms, treatment choices, expected duration, recovery/prognosis, ongoing care and recurrence. The quiz format, further-reading footer and review-date label have been removed from both teaching modes at the user's request.
- Labels and counselling are Chinese. Drug names, gene symbols, eponyms and useful technical abbreviations remain in their standard form.
- The teaching is an edited patient-facing summary, not a line-by-line translation. It replaces English excerpts and unsupported numerical promises with disease/procedure-specific guidance, follow-up and warning signs.
- The other clinical tabs retain their existing language. The corrections below also update those tabs where necessary to avoid contradictory advice.

## Focused clinical corrections

| Topic | Correction and supporting source |
| --- | --- |
| Retinal arterial occlusion | Emphasise immediate stroke-capable emergency assessment. Remove routine ocular massage/paracentesis advice and the arterial-occlusion backlinks from the paracentesis procedure. Reperfusion treatment remains a specialist protocol decision. [AHA statement summary](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/management-of-central-retinal-artery-occlusion/top-things-to-know). |
| Acute angle closure | Differentiate pupillary-block and secondary mechanisms; avoid indiscriminate pilocarpine and a universal fellow-eye/acute lens-surgery rule. [RCOphth guideline](https://www.rcophth.ac.uk/guidance-resources/management-of-angle-closure-glaucoma-exective-summary/), [College of Optometrists guidance](https://www.college-optometrists.org/clinical-guidance/clinical-management-guidelines/primaryangleclosure_primaryangleclosureglaucoma_pa). |
| Ocular syphilis | Retain the CDC IV penicillin regimen; clarify CSF indications, limited evidence for alternatives, and the lack of proven benefit of adjunctive systemic steroids. Remove the routine oral doxycycline alternative and claims of established Jarisch-Herxheimer prevention. [CDC guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/neurosyphilis.htm). |
| Chemical injury | Irrigate immediately with available clean water/saline; do not wait for a branded solution or claim universal superiority. Continue according to pH and retained particles. Avoid a rigid steroid stop date for every severity. [College of Optometrists guidance](https://www.college-optometrists.org/clinical-guidance/clinical-management-guidelines/trauma_chemical). |
| Retinitis pigmentosa supplements | Remove routine high-dose vitamin A advice. Distinguish treatment of a confirmed deficiency from unproven supplementation to slow inherited degeneration. [AAO technology assessment](https://www.aao.org/education/ophthalmic-technology-assessment/dietary-supplementation-retinitis-pigmentosa). |
| MacTel type 2 | Add the US FDA-approved ENCELTO option for adults, with specialist/local availability assessment. Distinguish it from an ordinary intravitreal injection and from Mendelian gene replacement. [FDA approval information](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/center-biologics-evaluation-and-research-cber-product-approval-information/encelto). |
| Intraocular gas | Remove universal positioning and fixed-duration clearance assumptions. Require pathology-specific posture and complete bubble absorption before flying/high-altitude travel or nitrous oxide. Include endothelial keratoplasty bubbles. [RCOphth safety alert](https://curriculum.rcophth.ac.uk/2018/12/ophthalmic-safety-alert-use-of-nitrous-oxide-when-there-is-gas-in-an-operated-eye/), [AAO flying advice](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/tips-prevention/can-i-fly-with-this-eye). |
| Intravitreal injection | Keep antisepsis central; do not routinely prescribe prophylactic topical antibiotics. Preserve urgent post-injection infection warnings. [AAO evidence discussion](https://www.aao.org/education/annual-meeting-video/antiseptics-vs-antibiotics-intravitreal-injections-2). |
| Sentinel-node biopsy | Remove automatic completion lymph-node dissection, universal tumour thresholds and fixed outcome percentages. Explain tumour-specific MDT decisions and the limits of extrapolating cutaneous melanoma evidence to periocular tumours. [NCI cutaneous melanoma guidance](https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/patient/melanoma-treatment-pdq). |
| Sebaceous carcinoma mapping | Remove automatic bilateral sampling, automatic exenteration for epithelial spread, and blanket rejection of all Mohs techniques. An epithelial-denuded sample is non-diagnostic. Add the missing sebaceous-carcinoma procedure backlink. [Map-biopsy study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3208231/), [ocular sebaceous-carcinoma treatment review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26147013/), [S1 guideline](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ddg.15405). |
| Inherited catalogue | Remove the same unsupported numerical prevalence estimate applied to every expanded entry. Distinguish optic neuropathy, retinal degeneration, syndromic disease, phenotype groupings and non-Mendelian MacTel. Uncertain variants or candidate associations are not treated as a definitive diagnosis. [GeneReviews assessment framework](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK1417/). |
| Terminology | Correct PAC in allergic conjunctivitis, the AC/A expansion in accommodative esotropia and ICG as a surgical dye. Distinguish continuous-wave versus micropulse cyclotherapy, and correct the skin-resurfacing laser explanation. |

Additional teaching checks cover warm-compress temperature, contact lenses and water, urgent retinal symptoms, glaucoma adherence, weak-vision rehabilitation, amblyopia after surgery, and avoidance of routine megadose steroids for indirect traumatic optic neuropathy. Useful sources are [CDC contact-lens advice](https://www.cdc.gov/contact-lenses/about/healthy-habits-keeping-water-away-from-contact-lenses.html), [AAO blepharitis guidance](https://www.aaojournal.org/article/S0161-6420%252818%252932645-9/fulltext), [AAPOS amblyopia advice](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/amblyopia), and [AAO traumatic optic neuropathy assessment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33161071/).

References are retained in this developer document and the relevant clinical corrections, not displayed as a footer in patient teaching. This was a focused review of the revised teaching and identified safety issues; it was not an exhaustive revalidation of every legacy dosage, prevalence estimate, staging system, device status or numerical outcome in the multi-megabyte clinical payload. Rare gene-disease relationships and variant interpretation still require condition-specific specialist references. US/UK approval or guideline statements do not establish Taiwan availability or replace local protocols.

## Expected-course counselling

`assets/quick_reference_patient_education_zh.js` explicitly assigns all 475 diseases and 192 procedures a counselling profile. Related records share profiles only where their treatment goals and expected course are comparable. The patient text separates symptom improvement, wound healing, visual recovery and long-term disease control. Time ranges are estimates, not permission to stop medication, resume contact lenses or disregard a gas bubble. Chronic and genetic disease counselling does not promise a short cure; diagnostic procedures explain that the eventual treatment depends on results.

Sources used to check representative recovery/course statements include [Moorfields corneal abrasion](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/corneal-abrasion), [CDC conjunctivitis](https://www.cdc.gov/conjunctivitis/treatment/), [NEI cataract surgery](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/cataracts/cataract-surgery), [Moorfields microbial keratitis](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/microbial-keratitis), [NHS blepharitis counselling](https://www.gloshospitals.nhs.uk/your-visit/patient-information-leaflets/blepharitis-ghpi0182/), [NHS retinal detachment](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/detached-retina-retinal-detachment/), [NEI eye injections](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/diabetic-retinopathy/injections-treat-eye-conditions), [BTS ocular tuberculosis statement](https://bmjopenrespres.bmj.com/content/9/1/e001225), [NHS optic neuritis](https://www.hey.nhs.uk/patient-leaflet/optic-neuritis/), [CUH giant cell arteritis](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/information-for-people-who-have-or-are-under-investigation-as-potentially-having-giant-cell-arteritis-gca/), [AAPOS sixth-nerve palsy](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/sixth-nerve-palsy), [AAPOS amblyopia](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/amblyopia), [AAPOS nasolacrimal obstruction](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/nasolacrimal-duct-obstruction), [Moorfields full-thickness corneal transplant](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/for-patients/information-hub/corneal-transplantation-penetrating-keratoplasty), [AAPOS strabismus surgery](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/strabismus-surgery) and [NHS eyelid surgery](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/cosmetic-surgery/eyelid-surgery/).

## Implementation and validation

### English Symptoms tab and conjunctivitis laterality

Every disease now opens on an English **Symptoms** tab placed before **Criteria**, using the existing English symptom field. `assets/quick_reference_symptoms_en.js` adds explicit laterality/fellow-eye notes for 23 conjunctival or related ocular-surface entries. The notes distinguish an initially unilateral infectious episode, common bilateral allergic/autoimmune disease, and local mechanical or medication exposure. Typical patterns are not absolute diagnostic rules. No universal percentage or fixed fellow-eye timeline is assigned; the approximate half-bilateral description for rare ligneous disease is identified as a published clinical estimate. Persistent unilateral scarring or inflammation warrants assessment for local causes or a masquerade.

| Entries / distinction | Supporting sources |
| --- | --- |
| Bacterial, adenoviral, allergic and paediatric conjunctivitis | [CDC symptom patterns](https://www.cdc.gov/conjunctivitis/signs-symptoms/index.html), [AAO bacterial conjunctivitis](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Bacterial_Conjunctivitis), [CDC viral clinical overview](https://www.cdc.gov/conjunctivitis/hcp/clinical-overview/). Ordinary bacterial disease may be unilateral, bilateral or sequentially bilateral; adenoviral disease often involves the fellow eye within days, whereas allergy is usually bilateral without contagious spread. Hyperacute gonococcal symptoms are explicitly separated from uncomplicated bacterial irritation. |
| Adult inclusion conjunctivitis versus trachoma | [College of Optometrists chlamydial conjunctivitis](https://www.college-optometrists.org/clinical-guidance/clinical-management-guidelines/conjunctivitis_chlamydial_adultinclusionconjunctiv), [AAO trachoma](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/diseases/what-is-trachoma). Adult inclusion is usually unilateral; trachoma typically affects both eyes. |
| Vernal, atopic and giant papillary disease; contact-lens exposure | [AAO vernal disease](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Vernal_Keratoconjunctivitis), [AAO atopic disease](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Atopic_Keratoconjunctivitis), [College of Optometrists CLAPC/GPC](https://www.college-optometrists.org/clinical-guidance/clinical-management-guidelines/cl-associatedpapillaryconjunctivitis_clapc_giantpa), [AAO GPC](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/diseases/what-is-giant-papillary-conjunctivitis). Distinguish bilateral allergic disease from a one-sided local trigger such as a prosthesis or exposed suture. |
| Sjögren dry eye, blepharoconjunctivitis, medication effects and superior limbic disease | [NEI dry-eye causes](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/dry-eye/causes-dry-eye), [NEI blepharitis](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/blepharitis), [AAO conjunctivitis differential table](https://www.aaojournal.org/article/S0161-6420%2818%2932646-0/fulltext), [AAO SLK teaching](https://www.aao.org/Assets/62378d36-5f0d-44ad-8f0c-c0bf1e57bbf6/637151444271870000/k7-pdf?inline=1). Noninfectious bilateral involvement is distinct from cross-infection; topical-drug laterality follows exposure. |
| Herpetic keratoconjunctivitis | [AAO HSV guideline](https://www.aao.org/education/clinical-statement/herpes-simplex-virus-keratitis-treatment-guideline), [AAO HZO](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Herpes_Zoster_Ophthalmicus). Usually unilateral, with important bilateral exceptions for HSV. |
| Microsporidial disease | [Clinical series of persistent/recurrent MKC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8302272/), [immunocompromised case series](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3170099/). Separate acute epithelial symptoms from slowly progressive stromal disease; bilateral involvement is possible. |
| Cicatricial MMP and SJS/TEN | [Moorfields ocular MMP](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/pemphigoid), [AAO SJS](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Stevens-Johnson_Syndrome). Bilateral involvement may be very asymmetric and is not contagious spread. The broader cicatricial category also includes unilateral local causes. |
| Ligneous and Parinaud oculoglandular disease | [AAO ligneous conjunctivitis](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Ligneous_Conjunctivitis), [AAO Parinaud oculoglandular syndrome](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Parinaud_Oculoglandular_Syndrome). Ligneous disease can involve either or both eyes; Parinaud oculoglandular disease is characteristically unilateral with ipsilateral nodes. |
| Neonatal disease | [CDC newborn conjunctivitis](https://www.cdc.gov/conjunctivitis/newborns/index.html), [AAO conjunctivitis differential table](https://www.aaojournal.org/article/S0161-6420%2818%2932646-0/fulltext). Presentation can occur beyond the first few days and laterality cannot identify the cause; urgent assessment is required regardless of whether one or both eyes are involved. |

### Teaching implementation

The **during treatment** phase now contains an ordered, procedure-specific sequence from `assets/quick_reference_procedure_steps_zh.js`: **192 interventions, 769 steps**. Closely equivalent duplicate catalogue entries share the same sequence (e.g. the two posterior-capsulotomy entries); there is no category-wide fallback. Descriptions explain the entry route, principal tissue changes, implant/graft placement and completion checks in patient-facing Traditional Chinese. They distinguish optional or alternative steps, staged reconstruction, historical/restricted procedures, diagnostic sampling and nonsurgical treatment. They do not prescribe an operator's dose, energy, cut depth, device setting or a universal surgical sequence.

The English method payload was used to identify the individual procedures, with patient-level sequence checks against these primary clinical services and sources:

- Cataract and lens surgery: [NEI cataract surgery](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/cataracts/cataract-surgery); specialist flanged fixation description: [Yamane et al., original technique](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28457613/).
- Corneal transplantation and surface procedures: [CUH corneal transplantation](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/corneal-transplantation/), [CUH cross-linking](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/corneal-cross-linking-for-keratoconus/), [Moorfields PTK](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/for-patients/information-hub/phototherapeutic-keratectomy-ptk-laser). DSAEK includes thin donor stroma, whereas DMEK uses the donor Descemet membrane/endothelium; both use bubble-assisted attachment rather than full-thickness graft fixation.
- Refractive surgery: [FDA SMILE patient information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf15/P150040S003C.pdf). The SMILE sequence describes femtosecond lenticule creation, dissection and removal, distinct from flap-based excimer ablation; no approval or local availability claim is inferred.
- Glaucoma: [NHS trabeculectomy explanation](https://www.gloshospitals.nhs.uk/your-visit/patient-information-leaflets/trabeculectomy-for-the-treatment-of-glaucoma/), [Moorfields glaucoma treatments](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/glaucoma/diagnosis-and-treatment), [AAPOS childhood glaucoma](https://aapos.org/glossary/glaucoma-in-children). Separate filtering, tube, canal/angle and cyclotherapy routes are described without copying legacy numerical settings.
- Retina, laser and injection: [Moorfields detachment surgery](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/retinal-detachment/diagnosis-and-treatment), [Moorfields vitreoretinal emergency service explanation](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/for-health-professionals/referring/vitreo-retinal-emergency-vre-clinic), [Moorfields injection treatment](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/age-related-macular-degeneration-amd/diagnosis-and-treatment), [Moorfields PDT](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/mediaLocal/pa4he4r2/photodynamic-therapy-pdt-_1.pdf). Preserve the distinction between scleral buckling outside the eye, vitrectomy inside the eye, bubble support, laser adhesion and photochemical PDT.
- Eyelid, lacrimal and socket surgery: [CUH ptosis repair](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/ptosis-correction-adults/), [CUH entropion repair](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/entropion-correction/), [CUH DCR](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/dacryocystorhinostomy-dcr/), [Moorfields eye removal](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/uveal-melanoma/removal-of-an-eye), [Moorfields prosthetic-eye service](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/for-patients/artificial-eye-creation-and-fitting). Reconstructive and donor sites, temporary eyelid bridging and later prosthesis fitting are kept distinct.
- Strabismus: [Moorfields squint surgery](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/strabismus-eye-squint/diagnosis-and-treatment), [AAPOS strabismus surgery](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/strabismus-surgery). Explain muscle exposure, repositioning/shortening and fixation, with later adjustment only for the selected adjustable method.
- Oncology and sampling: [NCI uveal melanoma](https://www.cancer.gov/types/eye/patient/intraocular-melanoma-treatment-pdq), [NCI retinoblastoma](https://www.cancer.gov/types/retinoblastoma/patient/retinoblastoma-treatment-pdq), [Moorfields ocular oncology guide](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/mediaLocal/zuacsidp/essential-adult-ocular-oncology-guide-20251210b.pdf), [CUH sentinel-node mapping and biopsy](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/sentinel-lymph-node-biopsy-for-melanoma/). Separate systemic, arterial and intraocular drug delivery; retain specialist anti-seeding safeguards and separate specimen/site tracking. Cutaneous sentinel-node process information is not used to impose universal periocular indications or completion-dissection rules.

Validation checks every intervention's ordered sequence and rendered steps, including representative distinctions for DSAEK/DMEK, scleral buckling, injection antisepsis, staged eyelid protection, SLNB, conjunctival mapping and SMILE.
