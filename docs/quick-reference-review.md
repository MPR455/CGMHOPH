# Quick Reference teaching review

Review date: 2026-10-25 (Asia/Taipei).

## Coverage and language

- All **475 disease entries** (377 main records and 98 expanded catalogue records) have Traditional Chinese teaching copy.
- All **192 intervention entries** have Traditional Chinese patient counselling centred on the treatment flow (preparation, what happens during treatment, and immediate follow-up), recovery time, prognosis and postoperative care. Repeat treatment and relevant risks are explained, with alternatives available in a collapsed section.
- Disease teaching explains causes/mechanisms, treatment choices, expected duration, recovery/prognosis, ongoing care and recurrence. The quiz format, further-reading footer and review-date label have been removed from both teaching modes at the user's request.
- Labels and counselling are Chinese. Drug names, gene symbols, eponyms and useful technical abbreviations remain in their standard form.
- The teaching is an edited patient-facing summary, not a line-by-line translation. It replaces English excerpts and unsupported numerical promises with disease/procedure-specific guidance, follow-up and warning signs.
- The other clinical tabs retain their existing language. The corrections below also update those tabs where necessary to avoid contradictory advice.

## Focused clinical corrections

| Topic | Correction and supporting source |
| --- | --- |
| Retinal arterial occlusion | Emphasise immediate stroke-capable emergency assessment. Remove routine ocular massage/paracentesis advice and the arterial-occlusion backlinks from the paracentesis procedure. Reperfusion treatment remains a specialist protocol decision. [AHA statement summary](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/management-of-central-retinal-artery-occlusion/top-things-to-know). |
| Acute angle closure | Differentiate pupillary-block and secondary mechanisms; avoid indiscriminate pilocarpine and a universal fellow-eye/acute lens-surgery rule. [RCOphth guideline](https://www.rcophth.ac.uk/guidance-resources/management-of-angle-closure-glaucoma-exective-summary/), [College of Optometrists guidance](https://www.college-optometrists.org/clinical-guidance/clinical-management-guidelines/primaryangleclosure_primaryangleclosureglaucoma_pa). |
| Ocular syphilis | Retain the CDC IV penicillin regimen; clarify CSF indications, limited evidence for alternatives, and the lack of proven benefit of adjunctive systemic steroids. Remove the routine oral doxycycline alternative and claims of established Jarisch-Herxheimer prevention. [CDC guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/neurosyphilis.htm). |
| Chemical injury | Irrigate immediately with available clean water/saline; do not wait for a branded solution or claim universal superiority. Continue according to pH and retained particles. Avoid a rigid steroid stop date for every severity. [College of Optometrists guidance](https://www.college-optometrists.org/clinical-guidance/clinical-management-guidelines/trauma_chemical). |
| Retinitis pigmentosa supplements | Remove routine high-dose vitamin A advice. Distinguish treatment of a confirmed deficiency from unproven supplementation to slow inherited degeneration. [AAO technology assessment](https://www.aao.org/education/ophthalmic-technology-assessment/dietary-supplementation-retinitis-pigmentosa). |
| MacTel type 2 | Add the US FDA-approved ENCELTO option for adults, with specialist/local availability assessment. Distinguish it from an ordinary intravitreal injection and from Mendelian gene replacement. [FDA approval information](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/center-biologics-evaluation-and-research-cber-product-approval-information/encelto). |
| Intraocular gas | Remove universal positioning and fixed-duration clearance assumptions. Require pathology-specific posture and complete bubble absorption before flying/high-altitude travel or nitrous oxide. Include endothelial keratoplasty bubbles. [RCOphth safety alert](https://curriculum.rcophth.ac.uk/2018/12/ophthalmic-safety-alert-use-of-nitrous-oxide-when-there-is-gas-in-an-operated-eye/), [AAO flying advice](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/tips-prevention/can-i-fly-with-this-eye). |
| Intravitreal injection | Keep antisepsis central; do not routinely prescribe prophylactic topical antibiotics. Preserve urgent post-injection infection warnings. [AAO evidence discussion](https://www.aao.org/education/annual-meeting-video/antiseptics-vs-antibiotics-intravitreal-injections-2). |
| Sentinel-node biopsy | Remove automatic completion lymph-node dissection, universal tumour thresholds and fixed outcome percentages. Explain tumour-specific MDT decisions and the limits of extrapolating cutaneous melanoma evidence to periocular tumours. [NCI cutaneous melanoma guidance](https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/patient/melanoma-treatment-pdq). |
| Sebaceous carcinoma mapping | Remove automatic bilateral sampling, automatic exenteration for epithelial spread, and blanket rejection of all Mohs techniques. An epithelial-denuded sample is non-diagnostic. Add the missing sebaceous-carcinoma procedure backlink. [Map-biopsy study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3208231/), [ocular sebaceous-carcinoma treatment review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26147013/), [S1 guideline](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ddg.15405). |
| Inherited catalogue | Remove the same unsupported numerical prevalence estimate applied to every expanded entry. Distinguish optic neuropathy, retinal degeneration, syndromic disease, phenotype groupings and non-Mendelian MacTel. Uncertain variants or candidate associations are not treated as a definitive diagnosis. [GeneReviews assessment framework](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK1417/). |
| Terminology | Correct PAC in allergic conjunctivitis, the AC/A expansion in accommodative esotropia and ICG as a surgical dye. Distinguish continuous-wave versus micropulse cyclotherapy, and correct the skin-resurfacing laser explanation. |

Additional teaching checks cover warm-compress temperature, contact lenses and water, urgent retinal symptoms, glaucoma adherence, weak-vision rehabilitation, amblyopia after surgery, and avoidance of routine megadose steroids for indirect traumatic optic neuropathy. Useful sources are [CDC contact-lens advice](https://www.cdc.gov/contact-lenses/about/healthy-habits-keeping-water-away-from-contact-lenses.html), [AAO blepharitis guidance](https://www.aaojournal.org/article/S0161-6420%252818%252932645-9/fulltext), [AAPOS amblyopia advice](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/amblyopia), and [AAO traumatic optic neuropathy assessment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33161071/).

References are retained in this developer document and the relevant clinical corrections, not displayed as a footer in patient teaching. This was a focused review of the revised teaching and identified safety issues; it was not an exhaustive revalidation of every legacy dosage, prevalence estimate, staging system, device status or numerical outcome in the multi-megabyte clinical payload. Rare gene-disease relationships and variant interpretation still require condition-specific specialist references. US/UK approval or guideline statements do not establish Taiwan availability or replace local protocols.

## Expected-course counselling

`assets/quick_reference_patient_education_zh.js` explicitly assigns all 475 diseases and 192 procedures a counselling profile. Related records share profiles only where their treatment goals and expected course are comparable. The patient text separates symptom improvement, wound healing, visual recovery and long-term disease control. Time ranges are estimates, not permission to stop medication, resume contact lenses or disregard a gas bubble. Chronic and genetic disease counselling does not promise a short cure; diagnostic procedures explain that the eventual treatment depends on results.

Sources used to check representative recovery/course statements include [Moorfields corneal abrasion](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/corneal-abrasion), [CDC conjunctivitis](https://www.cdc.gov/conjunctivitis/treatment/), [NEI cataract surgery](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/cataracts/cataract-surgery), [Moorfields microbial keratitis](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/microbial-keratitis), [NHS blepharitis counselling](https://www.gloshospitals.nhs.uk/your-visit/patient-information-leaflets/blepharitis-ghpi0182/), [NHS retinal detachment](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/detached-retina-retinal-detachment/), [NEI eye injections](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/diabetic-retinopathy/injections-treat-eye-conditions), [BTS ocular tuberculosis statement](https://bmjopenrespres.bmj.com/content/9/1/e001225), [NHS optic neuritis](https://www.hey.nhs.uk/patient-leaflet/optic-neuritis/), [CUH giant cell arteritis](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/information-for-people-who-have-or-are-under-investigation-as-potentially-having-giant-cell-arteritis-gca/), [AAPOS sixth-nerve palsy](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/sixth-nerve-palsy), [AAPOS amblyopia](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/amblyopia), [AAPOS nasolacrimal obstruction](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/nasolacrimal-duct-obstruction), [Moorfields full-thickness corneal transplant](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/for-patients/information-hub/corneal-transplantation-penetrating-keratoplasty), [AAPOS strabismus surgery](https://www.aapos.org/glossary/strabismus-surgery) and [NHS eyelid surgery](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/cosmetic-surgery/eyelid-surgery/).

## Implementation and validation

- `assets/quick_reference_teaching_zh.js` holds explicit Chinese explanations keyed by stable record IDs, renders escaped patient counselling and applies the documented clinical corrections before teaching is generated.
- `assets/quick_reference_patient_education_zh.js` supplies options/course/outlook/recurrence copy and selected plain-language mechanism explanations. Every intervention also has explicit preparation, immediate follow-up and aftercare text, separate from clinician-oriented technical notes. Missing assignments fail validation rather than silently using generic recovery advice.
- `quick_reference.html` loads that local asset, marks teaching panels `zh-Hant`, renders their structured HTML without splitting it into broken fragments, and includes Chinese teaching in both disease and intervention search. Search snippets strip HTML before matching.
- Run `node tools/check_quick_reference_teaching.cjs` for complete catalogue coverage, disease counselling and intervention flow/aftercare sections, absence of footer/date/quiz content, acute-versus-chronic course checks, residual English prose, Chinese search and focused clinical safety regressions.
- Headless Chrome checked all 667 panel DOMs, Chinese search, tab/record keyboard navigation, and 390 px mobile rendering with no horizontal overflow or browser/resource errors. Representative desktop and light/black mobile screenshots were visually inspected.
