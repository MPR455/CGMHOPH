# 11.3.7. Developmental Defects (VI): Ectopia Lentis (II)

## Hierarchy

- Hyperlysinemia
  - ectopia lentis
  - mental disability
  - muscular hypotony
- Persistent Fetal Vasculature
  - persistent hyperplastic primary vitreous (PHPV)
  - congenital
  - nonhereditary
  - unilateral
    - 90%
  - clinical presentation
    - lens
      - white, fibrous retrolental tissue
      - posterior cortical opacification
      - progressive cataract formation
    - elongated ciliary processes
    - prominent radial iris vessels
    - persistent hyaloid artery
- Ectopia Lentis et Pupillae
  - autosomal recessive
  - usually bilateral but not symmetric
  - the lens and the pupil are displaced in opposite directions
  - pupil
    - irregular
    - slit shaped
    - dilates poorly
    - displaced from the normal position
  - dislocated lens may bisect the pupil or may be completely luxated
  - associated ocular anomalies
    - severe axial myopia
      - similar to Marfan
    - retinal detachment
    - enlarged corneal diameter
    - cataract
    - abnormal iris transillumination
- Genetic Contributions to Age-Related Cataracts
  - a large proportion of the risk of age-related cataracts is inherited
  - cortical cataract
    - .>50% inherited
    - EPHA2 (gene associated with congenital and age-related cortical cataracts)
      - 1p36
      - accounts for only a small fraction of cortical opacities
      - the first gene known to cause hereditary, nonsyndromic age-related cortical cataracts
  - nuclear cataract
    - 35%–50% inherited
