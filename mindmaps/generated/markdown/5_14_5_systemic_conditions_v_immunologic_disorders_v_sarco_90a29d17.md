# 5.14.5. Systemic Conditions (V): Immunologic Disorders (V): Sarcoidosis

## Hierarchy

- Diagnosis and treatment
  - most patients with neurosarcoidosis exhibit abnormalities on MRI
    - 82%
      - meningeal and leptomeningeal enhancing lesion
        - most common
    - nonspecific
  - angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE)
    - elevated in 52–90%
      - if sarcoidosis is active!
  - gallium scan
    - not specific
  - high ACE + positive gallium scan
    - 100% specific and 73% sensitive
  - FDG-PET/CT
    - abnormal findings can also be present in malignancies
      - in particular lymphoma
  - histologic studies
    - conjunctiva, lymph nodes, or lung
    - noncaseating granulomas
  - treatment
    - corticosteroids
    - other immune modulatory therapy
- General
  - noncaseating granulomatous inflammation
  - young adults, aged 20–40 years
  - blacks : whites = 3 : 1
  - F>M
  - multisystem disease
    - eyes
      - lungs are involved most frequently
        - 30%–60% of cases may be discovered incidentally on a routine chest radiograph
    - liver
    - lymph nodes
    - skin
    - musculoskeletal system
    - nervous system
      - meningitis
        - 5%–15%
      - hydrocephalus
      - parenchymal involvement (most commonly hypothalamic)
      - encephalopathy
      - seizures
      - dural venous thrombosis
      - vasculitis
      - peripheral neuropathy
- Neuro-ophthalmic manifestations
  - facial nerve palsy
  - optic neuropathy
    - papillitis
      - most common cranial neuropathy
    - retrobulbar optic neuropathy
    - sarcoid granuloma at the optic nerve head
      - less common
    - neuroretinitis
      - disc swelling with a macular star of exudates
    - optic perineuritis
      - disc swelling without vision loss or increased intracranial pressure
    - papilledema
  - chiasmal and retrochiasmal visual pathway involvement
  - ocular motor cranial nerve palsy
  - gaze palsy
  - pupillary abnormalities
    - tonic pupil
    - Horner syndrome
    - Argyll Robertson pupils
- Intraocular manifestations
  - iritis
  - cataract
  - vitritis
  - retinal vasculitis (“candlewax drippings”)
  - chorioretinitis
