# Neovascular AMD (IV): Management II: Other Therapies

## Hierarchy

- Low vision therapies
  - implantable miniature telescope
    - magnification ≤ 2.7x
    - improves visual acuity by 2 lines
    - corneal decompensation due to endothelial cell loss
      - corneal transplant rate of 5% at 5 years
- Laser photocoagulation (“thermal laser”)
  - very rarely used
  - lesions sufficiently peripheral to foveal center with minimal risk of iatrogenic foveal damage and lower rate of recurrence
- overlay blank areas with small geometric figures or faces or any other image
- Low vision rehabilitation
  - visual effects of AMD
    - central scotomas
    - visual release hallucinations (Charles Bonnet syndrome)
  - magnification
    - high-plus lenses
    - video magnifiers
  - optimal lighting
  - contrast enhancement techniques
  - use of eccentric fixation outside the area of scotoma
    - adding PDT to ranibizumab reduced re-treatment rates but visual outcomes were inferior
- Photodynamic therapy (“cold laser”)
  - technique
    - intravenous administration of photo-sensitizing drug verteporfin
    - application of light of a specific wavelength
    - light incites localized photochemical reaction in targeted area
      - CNV thrombosis
    - rare
  - slows CNV progression
  - does not prevent significant vision loss in most eyes with CNV
  - upregulates VEGF in treatment area
  - rarely used
    - except for the most recalcitrant cases or for eyes with PCV
- Combination treatment
  - RADICAL
  - DENALI
  - adding radiation to ranibizumab therapy may reduce injection burden but visual outcomes are inferior
  - combination strategy (anti-VEGF + PDT) beneficial in some cases of PCV recalcitrant to anti-VEGF therapy alone
- Surgical treatments
  - intravitreal or subretinal tissue plasminogen activator injection with pneumatic displacement
    - for thick submacular hemorrhage
  - submacular surgery
    - removal of the CNV from beneath the fovea
  - macular translocation surgery
    - fovea is moved over to an area of healthier RPE
      - largely abandoned today
