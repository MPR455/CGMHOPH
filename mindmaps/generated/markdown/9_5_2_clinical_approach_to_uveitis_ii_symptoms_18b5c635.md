# 9.5.2. Clinical Approach to Uveitis (II): Symptoms

## Hierarchy

- Symptoms of Uveitis
  - acute-onset anterior uveitis
    - pain
      - ciliary spasm
      - secondary glaucoma
      - radiates over the trigeminal nerve area
    - photophobia
    - redness
    - blurred vision
    - epiphora
      - may not be associated with any symptoms
  - chronic anterior uveitis
    - blurred vision
      - calcific band keratopathy
      - cataract
      - CME
  - intermediate uveitis
    - floaters
    - blurred vision
      - CME
      - vitreous opacities
  - posterior uveitis
    - floaters
    - photopsia
    - metamorphopsia
    - scotomata
    - nyctalopia
    - painless decreased visual acuity
      - retinitis and/or choroiditis directly affecting macula
      - vitreous debris
      - opacities in the visual axis
        - inflammatory cells, fibrin, or protein in the anterior chamber
      - CME
      - epiretinal membrane
      - retinal ischemia
      - choroidal neovascularization
      - myopic or hyperopic shift
      - keratic precipitates
      - secondary cataract
      - macular edema
      - retinal atrophy
- Review of the patient’s health and other associated factors
  - See Table 5-10
    - Medical history factors
      - time course
      - onset
      - duration
      - severity
      - distribution
      - sex, age, race
      - immune system status
      - systemic medications
      - trauma history
      - travel history
      - social history
  - comprehensive history and review of systems
  - diagnostic survey for uveitis
  - ocular inflammation frequently does not correlate with inflammatory activity elsewhere in the body
  - uveitis may precede the development of inflammation at other body sites
